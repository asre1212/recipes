# Written recipe

The saved style for every recipe in this app:

- Warm ivory ruled notebook paper, a thin terracotta margin, and navy ink.
- Large italic, script-like headings; legible compact ingredient text.
- Concise steps with green circled numbers.
- Terracotta annotations and a separate margin-note area.
- Occasional hand-drawn dish-specific illustrations, spaced through the book; most pages have none.
- Quantities and instructions must come from supplied recipes. Show uncertain or missing details as unspecified; never infer bake settings or add-in quantities.
- Keep recipe content as accessible HTML. The supplied high-resolution card is the visual reference, not a substitute for readable text.

Cover: brown kraft notebook, white bear holding a chocolate-chip cookie. The app uses newly drawn SVG artwork. Original reference photographs and Library/account metadata are excluded from the public repository.


## Reference-matched detail sheet

The approved 2400 × 3100 cookie card is the layout target. `public/written-recipe.css` uses the local URW Z003 script and DejaVu Sans fonts, cream ruled sheet, pink margin and binder holes, a two-line cookie heading, rust hand-drawn underline, compact green ingredient bullets, outlined beige right margin notes, circled method numbers and a beige footer note. Navigation, category and edit controls sit outside the sheet. At phone width, the two-column ingredients/notes composition is retained where readable; very narrow screens stack it. Recipe text remains editable HTML.

The cover is a fresh vector tracing/recreation of the reference bear silhouette and pose on plain brown kraft. Its photograph is never part of the public source or deployment. Font licenses and source locations are bundled with the font assets.
